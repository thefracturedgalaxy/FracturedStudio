ABOUT FRACTURED STUDIOS

Fractured Studios is an independent development organization built around one simple idea: people should have the opportunity to create something meaningful together.

Our community combines development, training, creative work, and community management under one organization.

Developers can join departments, receive training, work on real projects, build their portfolios, and eventually take on greater responsibilities within the organization.

We are currently developing Fractured Paradox, while building the infrastructure and community needed for future projects.

OUR GOAL

Build a long-term development organization where talented people can learn, create, lead, and work on projects they can be proud of.

LEARN. BUILD. IMPROVE.

New contributors can enter as trainees and receive structured training before becoming full developers.

Training focuses on practical assignments rather than simply reading information. You learn by making things for real projects, with feedback from people who do the work.

Trainee›Junior Developer›Developer›Senior Developer›Lead›Supervisor

Progression depends on skill, reliability, contribution, and demonstrated ability.
